# Limitations, Negative Results, and Provenance

To preserve scientific transparency, this document records the exact denominators, clustering structure, statistical intervals, label provenance, negative results, and retractions from the accompanying study (*Correct Decisions, Wrong Evidence: The Hidden Fragility of AI Decisions Under Policy Change*).

## Key Denominators & Experimental Structure
- **Total Evaluation Calls vs. Inventory Records:** `4,972` total local authorization evaluation calls across `99` instantiated policy families using local 3.8B–8B Ollama models (`llama3.1:latest`, `qwen3:8b`, `gemma3:4b`, `phi4-mini:3.8b`).
- **Retrospective Semantic Corruption Audit (`authorization_audit_inventory.csv`):** `3,104` historical inventory records (`2,903` `FORMAL_PARSED` traces, `192` `UNVERIFIED_EX14` FAR rows, `9` `PARSE_FAILED` rows) spanning `364` synthetic cases, `69` instantiated policy families (`2` underlying structural forms across `E0`, `E1`, `E3`, `EX8`, `EX11`, `EX12`), and `6` schemas (`B5_A`–`B5_G`). Traces repeat across models and schemas and are clustered by family rather than independent.
  - **Model-Level Imbalance (`FORMAL_PARSED` / `v2-strict` Correct / Concealed Corruption):** `llama3.1:latest` (`1,375` / `784` / `231` = `29.46%`), `qwen3:8b` (`1,384` / `977` / `243` = `24.87%`), `phi4-mini:3.8b` (`72` / `53` / `12` = `22.64%`), `gemma3:4b` (`72` / `56` / `4` = `7.14%`).
  - **Decision-Class Breakdown (`490/1,870` = `26.2%`):** `ALLOW`: `12/518` (`2.3%`); `BLOCK`: `256/921` (`27.8%`); `ABSTAIN`: `222/431` (`51.5%`).
  - **Row-Level Wilson vs. Family-Clustered Bootstrap 95% CIs:** Descriptive row-level Wilson 95% CIs are `[1.3%, 4.0%]` (`ALLOW`), `[25.0%, 30.8%]` (`BLOCK`), and `[46.8%, 56.2%]` (`ABSTAIN`). Recomputed 10,000-replicate family-clustered bootstrap 95% CIs (`seed=20261115` over `69` families) are `[20.76%, 32.56%]` overall, `[0.85%, 3.98%]` (`ALLOW`, `65` families), `[18.76%, 37.33%]` (`BLOCK`, `69` families), and `[43.46%, 60.68%]` (`ABSTAIN`, `68` families).
- **Retrospective Override-Revocation Cohort (`override_revocation_cohort.csv`):** `288` `v2-strict`-correct overridden-prohibition `ALLOW` decisions: `10/10` corrupted-prohibition approvals (`B5_B`: `4`, `B5_A`: `2`, `B5_F`: `4`; `B5_G`: `0/36`) became unsafe `ALLOW`s vs. `0/278` clean approvals; single-field repair restored `10/10` to `BLOCK`.
- **Protocol-Defined Prospective Transition Evaluation (`EX-27B`, `protocol_ex27b.json`):** `24` template-generated families across `8` structural categories (`3` families/category) × `7` variants (`168` cases, `672` calls across `llama3.1:latest` and `qwen3:8b`). Phase-1 `B5_G` yielded `0/144` corrupted extractions, so deterministic clean replay predicted `0/96` unsafe approvals vs. `7/96` (`7.3%`, all on `V2_TRANSITION_U`) under fresh regeneration. Separately evaluated meaning-preserving updates (`V7_MEANING_M`, `N=48`) yielded `6/48` (`12.5%`). `12/13` fresh-regeneration failures stem from upstream temporal semantic binding past expiry across `3` temporal families (`EX27B-04`–`06`); excluding them leaves `1/42` (`2.4%`) on `V2` and `0/42` on `V7`.
- **External Benchmark Replication (`FOLIO / LINC`):**
  - `16,380` total released generations across `9` LINC `folio-neurosymbolic` runs (`182` examples, `67` source stories, `10` generations/example).
  - **Label Provenance & Ambiguity:** Classifications against `benlipkin/folio` gold FOLs are deterministic/AI-curated without independent human auditing (`HUMAN_AUDIT_NOT_DONE`). Across `348` majority-correct 8-shot model-example pairs, `50` are `CORRECT_ERRONEOUS` (`gpt-4-0613`: `17/132` = `12.88%`, story-clustered 95% CI `[6.50%, 20.49%]`; `gpt-3.5-turbo-16k-0613`: `11/114` = `9.65%`, `[3.08%, 17.76%]`; `starcoderplus`: `22/102` = `21.57%`, `[13.76%, 30.69%]`) and `22` are `CORRECT_AMBIGUOUS` (`18` `AMBIGUOUS_XOR_VS_OR` + `4` `BORDERLINE_OR_GOLD_CONVENTION`), preserved separately from `CORRECT_ERRONEOUS`.
  - **Missing-Quantifier Sensitivity Analysis:** Excluding `MISSING_QUANTIFIER` (`18/50` cases) yields `16/132` (`gpt-4-0613`), `7/114` (`gpt-3.5-turbo-16k-0613`), and `9/102` (`starcoderplus`), leaving `32/348` non-quantifier formalization errors while preserving the frozen `50/348` headline counts.
  - **Counterfactual Validation:** `286` eligible pairs across the 3 primary models (`62` ineligible solver/timeout pairs retained), yielding `12` retrospectively curated unambiguous masking events across `5` distinct source stories (`9/12` repaired by single-formula correction, `3/12` failed on secondary predicate mismatches), `2` separately disclosed `AMBIGUOUS_XOR_VS_OR` repairs on `EX 31`, and `12/13` faithful control agreement.

## Reproducibility Levels & Methodological Scope
1. **Offline Statistical Reproduction (`evidaudit reproduce`, `evidaudit verify`):** Recomputes all headline statistics directly from curated `benchmarks/data/` files with zero LLM calls and no theorem prover.
2. **Upstream FOLIO/LINC Prover Replay:** Requires the pinned `benlipkin/linc` checkout (`718dfe8fae342028a837a96096e5e1e412ad4f2e`) and NLTK `TableauProver`; wall-clock `signal.alarm` timeouts cause slight eligibility jitter (`286`–`290` eligible, `58`–`62` ineligible), while `benchmarks/data/folio_counterfactual_summary.json` preserves the frozen `286`/`62` snapshot.
3. **Independent Semantic Annotation Validation:** Offline reproduction does not independently validate AI-curated reference labels or semantic error classifications; independent human legal/logical auditing remains future work.

## Limitations & Preserved Negative Results
1. **Shared Closed-World Specification:** Authorization results are evaluated against specified reference policies and deterministic decision rules, where the authorization oracle, deterministic checkers, and declarative transition evaluator share one closed-world formal specification.
2. **Inconclusive Primary Cohorts:** The retrospective multi-blocker `BLOCK->BLOCK` cohort (`0/921`) and the prospective `C=1` corruption cohort (`0/144`) were empty, leaving those two specific hypotheses inconclusive.
3. **Retracted & Negative Prior Claims:** `14/18` earlier checker-collision claims were extraction-induced (`321/332` `CHECKER`-labeled errors were masked upstream extraction faults); strict schemas lower `ALLOW` recall by `-15.9` pp vs. direct judging and trail direct judging on authentic FAR prose (`58.3%` vs. `91.7%`).
4. **External Qualitative Transfer:** Logical QA (`TableauProver` over FOLIO) is not an authorization gate; prevalence rates do not transfer directly to production access-control systems.
