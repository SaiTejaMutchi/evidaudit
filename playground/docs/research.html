Research Foundation

Scientific continuity connecting IEEE ICTAI 2026 ContractNLI retrieval sufficiency, Apart AI x Epistemics 2026 semantic integrity, and EvidAudit.

EvidAudit operationalizes findings from two research studies:

  • Correct Decisions, Wrong Evidence: The Hidden Fragility of AI Decisions Under Policy Change (2026). Across 2,903 clustered local authorization traces (364 synthetic cases, 69 policy families) and 16,380 released LINC/FOLIO traces (348 majority-correct model-example pairs audited), 26.2% (490/1,870) of strict-checker-correct decisions consumed corrupted intermediate facts or ungrounded citations, matched frozen replay missed fresh-regeneration failures (0/96 vs. 7/96, plus 6/48 meaning-preserving updates; 12/13 temporal in 3 families), and external auditing uncovered 12 counterfactual masking events across 5 source stories (9/12 repaired).
  • When Does Retrieval Still Matter? Operating Regimes for Full-Document Reasoning: Evidence from ContractNLI (IEEE ICTAI 2026, accepted and presented). Across 2,091 ContractNLI pairs in 123 contracts, 82.72% of agentic retrieval expansions produced no final prediction change while missing evidence explained retrieval errors.
